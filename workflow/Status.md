## Accepted Decisions, Proposals, & Deferred Scope

DP1 to DP3 are the three Layer 1 data pipelines. GX is Great Expectations. SCD2 is a type 2 slowly changing dimension, which keeps every version of a record.

| Subject | State | Choice & Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business domain & sources** | Decided | Use the TPC-DI retail brokerage domain and all 18 business and reference source families. Exclude benchmark-only audit metadata (`<name>_audit.csv`, `Audit` table) and `BatchDate.txt`. |
| **Run parameters** | Decided | Four inclusive UTC dates: `historical_start`, `historical_end`, `incremental_start`, and `incremental_end`. `incremental_start` must be the day after `historical_end`; a configuration that breaks this rule is rejected. The historical load's `Prospect.csv` snapshot is dated `historical_end` ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#the-firm)). |
| **Calendar & load cycle** | Decided | The historical period covers 2014-01-01 to 2018-12-31 and arrives as one historical load (TPC-DI's Batch 1). The incremental period covers 2019-01-01 to 2025-12-31, with one daily batch per UTC calendar date, weekends and holidays included (2,557 daily batches). The cutover is the switch from 2018-12-31 to 2019-01-01. One UTC clock without daylight saving time; the real NYSE holiday calendar ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#the-firm)). |
| **Source naming & completeness** | Decided | `historical/` keeps standard names. Daily files use `incremental/YYYYMMDD/<Source>_YYYYMMDD.<ext>`; FINWIRE files keep their quarter names. These are the paths in `upstream`; `staging` adds a version component (layout open, area 2). An expected-file list per delivery decides completeness; a zero-record file means no activity. |
| **Source cadences** | Decided | `Prospect.csv`: a full snapshot on the first day of each month. FINWIRE: each quarter is released on the last day of the following month. `HR.csv` and the six reference files: loaded once, then static ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#core-entities)). |
| **Cash data identity** | Decided | Every cash movement carries `CT_ID`, one global sequence from the source system: the first field of `CashTransaction.txt` and the event ID of stream messages. Duplicates keep it. `CT_NAME` uses fixed forms that DP2 and Flink Job 1 parse into `cash_type` and `trade_id` ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#events-and-consistency-rules)). |
| **Generation model** | Decided | Four generation layers: business behavior, attrition signal, source recording, and delivery. The generator runs layers 1 to 3; the publisher runs layer 4. Both live in `data/data_source/` (`generator/` and `publisher/`), the simulated source department. The generator extends the Polars POC with a day-by-day simulation ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#the-generation-model)). |
| **Simulated firm (generation layer 1)** | Decided | A mid-size US retail brokerage: at scale factor 100, about 330,000 customers and 550,000 accounts at cutover, growing about 8% per year. Five hidden behavior profiles, hidden wealth, about 20% margin accounts, tier from customer wealth, four cash kinds including dividends, and no short selling ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#layer-1-business-behavior)). |
| **Scale factor** | Decided | The `scale_factor` run parameter scales only the firm: customers, accounts, prospects, brokers, and all their activity. Market and reference data and the calendar are separate settings. One unit is about 1 GB of offline files over the reference calendar (2014 to 2025); any time slice gets its share of that volume, so a shorter calendar produces less data from the same firm. Standard factors: 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 50, and 100; factor 100 is the designed firm. The customers per unit (about 3,300) are calibrated after the first measured runs ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#scale-factor)). |
| **Generated volume & processed workload** | Decided | The offline files alone must reach at least 100 GB (rubric row `r5`), so the factor 100 dataset is generated once and its offline files must measure at least 100 GB; streamed cash (about 18 GB) is counted and reported separately. The design handles larger scales by principle (see the scaling path); each environment, such as this machine or a later cloud deployment, runs the largest factor it handles well, and each experiment processes only as much as shows its effect ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#11-workload)). |
| **Attrition signal (generation layer 2)** | Decided | Closure risk depends on profile, tenure, tier, and recent losses; about 3% of accounts close per year. 80% of closures follow a hidden leaving period of 1 to 8 weeks. About 2% of accounts per year show look-alike outflows and stay ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#layer-2-attrition-signal)). |
| **Source recording (generation layer 3)** | Decided | TPC-DI's own data errors only. From 2019-07-01 (a setting), `Trade.txt` carries a 15th field, `T_CHANNEL`; this schema change is our addition ([details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#layer-3-source-recording)). |
| **Delivery (generation layer 4)** | Decided | The publisher replays one generated dataset with a named publisher profile, such as `clean` or `realistic`. All rates are configuration; the realistic defaults are in the [details](records/Data-Generation-Draft.md#layer-4-delivery). |
| **Source boundary & buckets** | Decided | The source systems belong to another department (rubric row `r8`). Buckets: `upstream` (source side), `staging` (the platform's copy of every pulled file version), `lakehouse`, and `platform`. DP1 pulls from `upstream`, which the platform can only read. The source side owns the raw cash topic ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#2-source-boundary-and-storage)). |
| **Layer 1 tools** | Decided | Delta Lake for Bronze and Silver, Spark, Gold in PostgreSQL built by dbt-postgres, Trino, Airflow 3, GX Core with DataHub, Kafka with Kafbat UI, and Flink 2.2. GX Core replaces Soda; pg_duckdb is set aside ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#3-layer-1-tools)). |
| **Versions** | Decided | Start with Spark 3.5 and Delta 3.3, plus Flink 2.2 with the Delta 4.4 Flink connector. An end-to-end smoke test confirms them: Flink writes a Delta table on SeaweedFS, then Spark and Trino read it. Spark 4 with Delta 4 is the fallback. |
| **Airflow pipelines** | Decided | DP1: `upstream` to `staging` to Bronze. DP2: Bronze to Silver, then the rows Gold needs into PostgreSQL, then dbt. DP3: feature tables. Each has an ingest stage and a GX validate stage that report to DataHub ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#41-pipelines)). |
| **Bronze tables** | Decided | One table per source; every field as delivered text, plus metadata columns (the set is open, area 2). Rebuilt from `staging`, or from the raw topic for stream deliveries, when needed ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#42-bronze-tables)). |
| **Late files & load status** | Decided | A table waits only for its own file and the tables it depends on. `ops.load_status` and a completeness view show what each date is missing, and dashboards label partial dates ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#43-dependencies-and-late-files)). |
| **Data errors & quarantine** | Decided | Bronze keeps raw values. DP2 repairs a value only when the correct value is certain; otherwise it keeps the record and adds quality metadata. Unusable records go to quarantine, and no value is ever invented ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#44-data-errors-and-quarantine)). |
| **Schema evolution handling** | Decided | Two versioned `Trade.txt` contracts. DP1 detects the version by field count; Bronze stores each version as delivered, and Delta schema merge adds the new column at the first version 2 write; Silver maps both versions to one schema. The baseline's fixed schema drops the new field without an error ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#45-schema-evolution)). |
| **Gold model** | Decided | The warehouse model has 19 tables: TPC-DI's 19 minus `DImessages`, plus `fact_cash_transactions`. dbt builds `fact_cash_balances` daily from `fact_cash_transactions`. `fact_market_history` keeps the price-to-earnings ratio (P/E), 52-week values, and yield ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#47-gold-model-changes)). |
| **Stream processing** | Decided | Two Flink 2.2 jobs chained through the clean topic. Job 1: raw topic to Bronze and Silver, `CT_ID` deduplication, and a progress marker for DP2. Job 2: live tables, hourly and daily windows, feature rows, and rescoring requests ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#5-stream-processing)). |
| **Reruns & corrections** | Decided | Every write is safe to repeat. A rerun covers the affected `data_date` range and every later date, in order. `staging` and Bronze keep every delivery version. Feature corrections replace rows, and MLflow versions each training dataset with its model ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#6-reruns-corrections-and-versioning)). |
| **Data versioning baseline** | Decided | Every pulled file version in `staging` and Bronze, provenance in Bronze, Delta table history, and SCD2 history in Gold ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#62-rules)). |
| **ML problem: account attrition** | Decided | Predict whether an account closes within a horizon; a tier threshold on the probability creates a broker outreach task. Scoring runs daily for all open accounts, and again for one account after a large withdrawal. This replaces the minimum-cash forecast ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#8-layer-2-problem-account-attrition)). |
| **ML features** | Decided | In the incremental period, cash reaches the platform only through Kafka. Flink Job 2 produces the cash features, DP3 produces the others, and each feature has one producer. The rubric asks for offline and streaming features, not for a streaming model ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#82-the-problem)). |
| **Build approach** | Decided | Spec first: `README.md` and `docs/` describe the target system, we build to it, and we update the docs when a decision changes. Only decided items go into `docs/`. `data/gx/` replaces `data/soda/`. |
| **Workspace, docs, & 4-pillar repo** | Decided | Separate `docs/` (5 public system docs, plus the TPC-DI specification and coursework files as references), `workflow/` (status, guides, `check_docs.py`, `diagrams/`), and `local/` (gitignored scratch and archives); organize code into `data/`, `ml/`, `agents/`, and `infra/` without a root `Makefile` or root `tests/` ([Workflow Overview](README.md#5-design-decisions-and-rationale-for-this-system)). |
| **Schema & table naming convention** | Decided | Enforce `snake_case` with rubric prefixes (`bronze.raw_*`, `silver.stg_*`, `gold.dim_*`, `gold.fact_*`, `gold.feat_*`) and `_snake_case` metadata columns across the system ([Workflow Overview](README.md#3-unified-naming-conventions)). |
| **Scoring & serving flow** | Proposed | The daily run sends batched requests to KServe; every path uses the MLflow `production` model; keep each account's latest score plus full history for flagged accounts ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#82-the-problem)). |
| **Data quality ownership** | Proposed | Tag each check as business or technical and route it to its owner. Business owners use DataHub plus business intelligence (BI) dashboards and data access; engineers use the tool UIs and Grafana ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#7-data-quality-and-governance)). |
| **Observability direction** | Proposed | Prometheus metrics from every service, OpenTelemetry for traces and logs, and Grafana as the one dashboard; tool UIs stay for debugging ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#9-cross-cutting-concerns)). |
| **Maintenance jobs** | Parked | They wait until the base system works. Sketch: a separate Airflow pipeline (DAG) compacts the Flink-written Delta tables while Job 1 is paused ([details](records/Platform-Design-Draft.md#55-operations)). |
| **Out of scope** | Excluded | Manual business edits; extraction from a simulated trading database with change data capture (a novel-idea candidate); a text encoding problem; a `CT_NAME` format-change problem; ongoing HR and reference updates. |
